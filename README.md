# SC Memories Saver

SC Memories Saver is the easiest way to back up and preserve your Snapchat memories — keeping your photos and videos saved in the correct order, with the right dates, locations, and original quality.

Never worry about losing your memories again. SC Memories Saver helps you safely move your Snapchat photos and videos into your iOS Photos app, organized just the way you remember them.

## Two Smart Processing Modes

### 1. Accurate Mode (Recommended)
- Creates fresh, clean copies of your memories with:
  - Correct dates and times
  - Accurate locations (when available)
  - Original image and video quality
- Best for long-term backups and preserving memories exactly as they happened.

### 2. Fast Mode
- Quickly extracts your memories directly from your Snapchat export ZIP file.
- Perfect when you just want everything saved fast.

## How It Works

1. **Request your Snapchat Memories export**
   - Open Snapchat → Settings → Privacy → Export your data
   - Select "Memories" and request your data
   - Wait for Snapchat to prepare your backup (usually 24-48 hours)
   - Download the ZIP file(s)

2. **Choose your preferred processing mode**
   - Select Accurate Mode for best quality and metadata preservation
   - Or choose Fast Mode for quick extraction

3. **Select the exported ZIP file**
   - Import the ZIP file(s) from your downloads
   - The app will extract and organize your memories

4. **Sit back — SC Memories Saver organizes and saves everything to your Photos app**
   - Browse your memories in the gallery
   - Export selected memories or save everything at once
   - Your memories are preserved with all their original details

## Troubleshooting

### Choosing Between Modes
- **Accurate Mode (Recommended)** - Creates fresh, clean copies with verified dates, times, and locations. Best for long-term backups and preserving memories exactly as they happened.
- **Fast Mode** - Quickly extracts memories directly from ZIP. Perfect when you just want everything saved fast.

### "Could not read the ZIP file" error
- Ensure you downloaded the ZIP directly from Snapchat
- Try importing one ZIP file at a time
- Check that the file isn't corrupted

### Accurate mode is slow
- This is normal! Downloads are verifying each memory from Snapchat's servers
- Speed depends on your internet connection and number of memories
- Try Fast mode if you need quick processing

### "Product not available" when trying to unlock premium
- Make sure you have an active internet connection
- Restart the app
- Check that you're signed into the App Store
