# SC Memories Saver

SC Memories Saver is the easiest way to back up and preserve your SC memories — keeping your photos and videos saved in the correct order, with the right dates and original quality.

Never worry about losing your memories again. SC Memories Saver helps you safely move your photos and videos from your data export into your iOS Photos app, organized and preserved.

## How It Works

SC Memories Saver processes your data export ZIP file locally on your device:
- Extracts your photos and videos with metadata preservation
- Maintains chronological order and original dates
- Works completely offline - no internet required
- All processing happens on your device for maximum privacy

## Usage

1. **Request your data export**
   - Open the social app → Settings → Privacy → Export your data
   - Select "Memories" and request your data
   - Wait for the service to prepare your backup (usually 24-48 hours)
   - Download the ZIP file(s)

2. **Select your exported ZIP file in the app**
   - Import the ZIP file(s) from your downloads
   - The app will extract and organize your memories locally

3. **Browse and save to your Photos app**
   - View your memories in the gallery
   - Select which memories to save or export everything at once
   - Your memories are preserved with their original details

## Troubleshooting

### "Could not read the ZIP file" error
- Ensure you downloaded the ZIP directly from your data export
- Try importing one ZIP file at a time
- Check that the file isn't corrupted

### Processing takes a long time
- Large exports with thousands of memories can take 15-30 minutes
- This is normal for batch processing
- Keep the app in the foreground for best performance

### "Product not available" when trying to unlock premium
- Make sure you have an active internet connection
- Restart the app
- Check that you're signed into the App Store
